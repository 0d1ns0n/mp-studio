MP Studio

MP Studio is a personal desktop tool that produces short vertical videos and publishes them to the creator's own accounts (YouTube, Telegram, TikTok) at the creator's request.

The creator connects their own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit and chooses for each finished video whether to send it to TikTok. Videos are AI-generated and are labeled as AI-generated content when posted.
