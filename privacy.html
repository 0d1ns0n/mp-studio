Privacy Policy — moneyPrint Studio

Last updated: November 14, 2026

What the app accesses

  • Basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name) to show which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload or publish videos that the user explicitly chooses to send to their own TikTok account.
  • Statistics of the user's own public videos (views, likes, comments, shares) to show the user how their videos perform.

Where data is stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the user's own computer. The app has no servers, does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

Deleting data

The user can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions → Apps and services) and delete the local token files.
